# TODO_LEGAL — Legal and Regulatory Constraints

## AI-Generated Content
- Acureal uses Claude for synthesis, narrative, and next-step recommendations
- All Claude outputs are advisory, not legal opinions
- Users must engage qualified legal counsel for title verification, property law, and regulatory compliance
- UI must never present AI output as definitive legal or regulatory authority

## Document Extraction Accuracy
- Gemini extraction may not be 100% accurate for handwritten, low-quality scanned, or heavily regional-language documents
- Human review is required before relying on extracted field values for binding decisions
- Extraction confidence scores are shown to guide review, not replace it

## Zoning and Planning Rules
- Planning zones, FAR/FSI, and building regulations are authority-specific, version-sensitive, and subject to change
- Acureal surfaces rule-set content sourced from uploaded documents only
- Never present unversioned or unattributed zoning data as authoritative
- Effective date and source document must be recorded for every rule
- Treat uploaded zoning/master-plan documents as source material, not legal advice
- Add legal review or specialist review steps wherever draft-vs-live ambiguity exists

## Comps and Market Data
- Market benchmarks and transaction evidence are synthesized from user-uploaded sources
- Acureal does not scrape government registry data
- Confidence levels and source types are always surfaced
- No market output should be treated as a formal valuation

## RERA Compliance
- RERA compliance requirements vary by state, project type, and phase
- Acureal tracks RERA numbers and documents as provided by users
- Verification of RERA registration status must be done against the official K-RERA / RERA portal
- Confirm lawful workflow for Karnataka RERA verification beyond manual entry and uploaded evidence before enabling any automated status display

## Registry / Encumbrance Access
- Confirm whether Kaveri / sub-registrar data can be accessed through any approved API, partner feed, or manual process
- Keep uploaded EC-driven extraction as the only supported source until a lawful path is confirmed

- Do not imply live registry connectivity until a lawful, stable source is available

## Portal and Partner API Boundaries
- BBMP e-Aasthi / e-Khata, Kaveri, Bhoomi RTC, and RERA portal checks must remain deep-link and user-upload workflows unless written API permission, partner terms, or official documentation is obtained
- Do not automate CAPTCHA, OTP, login-gated, or session-bound citizen portal flows
- Vendor data, including Landeed or similar providers, must be labeled as vendor data until reviewed and accepted into Acureal evidence tables
- K-GIS geometries and hierarchy lookups are reference context only; do not present them as survey demarcation or legal boundary certification
- Record source URL, provider, retrieval timestamp, confidence, and reviewer before promoting any extracted portal/vendor fact into verified intelligence

## Data Privacy
